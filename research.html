Research tracks

Vision-language domain generalization

Fine-tuning Qwen3VL-2B, Qwen2.5VL-3B, and SmolVLM2-2.2B with LoRA on held-out video domains to study how well VLMs transfer to domains unseen during training. Includes cross-domain gradient-similarity diagnostics to quantify where and why generalization breaks down.

Tsetlin Machine interpretability

Building clause-sanitation pipelines that identify and remove non-contributing clauses from trained Tsetlin Machines, quantifying the accuracy impact of pruning and testing composite clause-weighting strategies across MNIST, Fashion-MNIST, and synthetic datasets.

Publications

A rule-based and firing-rate filtering pipeline that reconstructs trained Tsetlin Machine clause sets from stored weights, isolates clauses that contribute little to classification decisions, and measures the resulting change in accuracy when they're removed — a step toward more interpretable, compact TM models.
A split Tsetlin Machine pipeline that combines top and bottom clause pairs via a Cartesian product across five weighting strategies, with composite accuracy experiments and Spearman correlation analysis across MNIST, Fashion-MNIST, and synthetic shape datasets.
